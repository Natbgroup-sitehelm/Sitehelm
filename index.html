Common questions

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need to install anything? ▾
No installation required. SiteHelm is four HTML files. Put them in a folder on your computer and open the main one in Chrome. No server, no IT department, no app store download.
How long does setup take? ▾
Most parks are operational within the same day. We schedule a 45-minute onboarding call where we set up your sites on the map, configure your pricing, add your staff, and do a test booking together. By the end of the call you're live.
What happens to my data if I cancel? ▾
Your data is stored in your browser's local storage — it stays on your machine. You can export a full backup at any time from the Backup tab. If you cancel, your data goes with you. We recommend taking a backup before cancelling.
Can I use SiteHelm on multiple devices? ▾
Yes — using the built-in backup and restore system to move data between computers. For real-time multi-device sync, we're rolling out cloud database support shortly. Contact us to be on the early access list.
Do guests need to download an app to book? ▾
No. The guest booking page is a standard web page that works on any phone, tablet, or computer. Guests just visit your URL, pick their dates, choose a site, and pay. No app, no account, no friction.
How does the ANPR vehicle recognition work? ▾
A camera at your entrance captures vehicle plates on motion trigger. The image is sent to a cloud AI service that reads the plate text in under 500ms. SiteHelm matches it against your active bookings and logs the entry. Unknown vehicles trigger an instant SMS to your phone. We recommend Hikvision cameras and partner with local security installers for the hardware setup.
Is Australian support available? ▾
Yes — all support is handled directly by the SiteHelm team in Australia. Not a call centre, not a ticketing system. For the first 90 days after onboarding you have direct access to your account manager's number.
What if I'm not tech savvy? ▾
SiteHelm was designed for park operators, not IT professionals. We provide a full 16-section printed user manual, a personal onboarding session, and ongoing direct support. If you can use a website, you can use SiteHelm.
